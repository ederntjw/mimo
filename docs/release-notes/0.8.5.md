## Mimo 0.8.5

Mimo now has a secure, built-in update channel and a more balanced Live Meeting launcher.

### Updates that feel like a Mac app

- Mimo checks its signed update feed automatically once per day.
- Check for Updates remains available from the menu bar whenever you want to check manually.
- New versions show their release notes, download inside Mimo, install, and relaunch through Sparkle.
- Every update DMG is verified against Mimo's dedicated EdDSA signing key before installation.

### Live Meeting polish

- The Start Live Meeting action is optically centered, with its arrow kept independently at the trailing edge.
- The launcher fills the available meeting content width evenly at compact and wide window sizes.
- Strawberry Milk styling remains consistent across the launcher, live summary, Q&A, and recording state.

### Live assistant baseline

- Live Meeting opens directly into Live Summary.
- The first brief can arrive after about 12 seconds of committed speech and refreshes about every 30 seconds.
- Ask Mimo continues to answer from the accumulated transcript without interrupting local transcription or recording.
