## Mimo 0.8.8

### Keep asking after a meeting

- Ask Mimo remains available while a meeting finishes processing and after it is complete.
- Questions use the selected meeting’s transcript and recent conversation to understand follow-up questions. Each meeting keeps its own conversation during the app session.
- Answers already in progress can finish after recording stops. Questions about a saved meeting do not include live captions from another meeting.

### Choose a model for re-transcription

- Re-transcribe now opens a model chooser for saved recordings, including full Whisper Large v3, Whisper Large Turbo, and compatible downloaded models.
- Model descriptions and download status help you choose. The selection applies only to that re-transcription and does not change your default model.
- Re-transcription replaces the transcript and regenerates the summary while retaining manual notes and the saved audio.
- Active recordings, transcription jobs, and meetings that resume while the chooser is open are protected from overlapping re-transcription.
- Choose Save Recording when prompted at the end of a meeting to make later re-transcription available.

### Reliability

- Fixed a Quill request parameter rejected by the ChatGPT Codex endpoint while preserving output limits for other transports.
- Added a targeted Swift 6.4 compiler workaround for the transcription dispatcher; inference remains optimized inside the speech backends.
