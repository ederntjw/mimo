## Mimo 0.9.0 — Bilingual Flow

### One setup for Chinese and English

- New installations use full Whisper Large v3 on this Mac for dictation, live meeting batches, and final transcription, with automatic language detection and no model picker.
- Cleanup, meeting notes, questions, and Quill use your connected ChatGPT subscription. Speech recognition works offline after the model is prepared; writing features need internet access and available account usage.
- Existing installations retain their current providers. Choose the simple Chinese–English setup from Models to opt in after reviewing how text is sent to ChatGPT.
- Full Whisper Large v3 is about a 3.1 GB download. At least 16 GB Mac RAM is recommended, preferably 24 GB; accuracy and processing time vary with audio and hardware.

### A calmer workspace

- Refreshed typography, warm Classic colours, clearer navigation, and a rounded content area without hard outer dividers.
- Simplified settings group everyday controls into General, Dictation, Meetings, Privacy, and Appearance.
- Dictionary, shortcuts, recording preferences, permissions, and existing sync controls remain accessible.

### Make dictation your own

- Choose None, Light, or Medium cleanup. Light is the default for the simple setup and preserves Chinese–English wording while tidying fillers, punctuation, and obvious grammar.
- Recover and copy the original text for new dictations when cleanup or a snippet changes it. Older history remains unchanged.
- Create spoken snippets for signatures, addresses, and frequent replies. Say the saved phrase on its own to insert its text exactly as written.

### Privacy and installation

- Speech audio is processed on this Mac in the simple setup. Cleanup and notes send text to ChatGPT; enabled app/screen context can be included. Optional Mimo Account sync is controlled separately.
- Existing settings, history, retained recordings, and credentials are preserved. No existing speech model files are removed.
- Download `Mimo-0.9.0.dmg` for an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 14.2 or later. The public app and disk image are Developer ID signed, Apple notarized, and stapled; Sparkle updates use the existing signing key.

Mimo remains independently implemented open-source software under the MIT license, with bundled font and dependency notices preserved.
